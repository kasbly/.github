## What we build

[Kasbly](https://kasbly.com) is a multi-tenant AI automation platform for small and mid-sized commerce teams. One AI employee handles the work that usually needs three people:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Create & publish** | Turns a single product photo into a week of on-brand content: product shots, UGC-style video and copy, scheduled and auto-published across channels. [Learn more →](https://kasbly.com/en/products/create) |
| **Sell & support** | One inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS and web chat, with qualification, CRM write-back and checkout inside the conversation. [Learn more →](https://kasbly.com/en/products/sell) |

Built for **Arabic and English** markets, with native support for regional commerce platforms including Salla, Zid, Shopify and WooCommerce.
