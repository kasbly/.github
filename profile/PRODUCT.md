# Kasbly

**Website:** https://kasbly.com

The AI employee that replaces your marketing and sales team.

**One desk. Many accounts. Each one a different person. Then it stays in the chat until the order is paid.**

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kasbly Marketing** | Connect every account, each with its own persona. AI publishes across all of them. |
| **Kasbly Sales** | When customers reply, Kasbly Sales stays in the conversation until it closes and creates the order in your store. |
